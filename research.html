Medical Computer Vision Lab

Research

My research focuses on a practical question in medical AI: before trying to prevent catastrophic forgetting, can the model reliably learn each clinical task in the first place? I use continual abdominal-trauma CT detection to study how preprocessing, representation quality, labels, and task formulation interact with sequential learning.

Current work

Learning to Window: Context-Aware HU Adaptation for Continual Trauma Injury Detection in CT Scans

Fixed CT windows encode a preprocessing choice before the network sees the image. This project asks whether that choice should instead be part of the learning problem, especially when a model must acquire abdominal-trauma tasks sequentially.

Question
Can task-adaptive Hounsfield Unit preprocessing improve continual trauma detection while preserving previously learned knowledge?
Approach
Differentiable CT windowing combined with sequential organ-level training and continual-learning baselines.
Diagnosis
Ablations separate the effects of broad versus narrow initialization, hard clipping versus smooth transforms, window learnability, and task-specific adaptation.
Goal
Determine which gains come from the continual-learning method itself and which come from making the imaging representation more learnable.

02

Diagnostic continual-learning study

Accepted · MICAD 2026
Co-lead & corresponding author

What Limits Continual Learning for Abdominal-Trauma CT Detection? A Diagnostic Study of Forgetting vs. Representation Bottlenecks

Rather than assuming catastrophic forgetting is the only failure mode, this work follows a staged diagnostic sequence: a custom CNN, a pretrained ResNet-18, and patient-level attention-based multiple-instance learning. The experiments compare fine-tuning, EWC, replay, EWC+replay, and LwF under class-incremental and window-based domain-incremental settings.

The central contribution is methodological: continual-learning performance is interpreted only after checking whether the representation, label granularity, and patient-level formulation make the underlying tasks learnable.

03

Research direction

Diagnose before optimizing

Separate catastrophic forgetting from failure caused by weak representations, label mismatch, class imbalance, or insufficient task learnability.

Make preprocessing learnable

Study differentiable and context-aware intensity transformations instead of treating clinical preprocessing as a fixed upstream decision.

Move toward heterogeneous clinical data

Extend adaptive learning beyond CT toward multimodal settings that combine images with demographic, physiological, and structured clinical information.

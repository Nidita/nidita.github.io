Preliminary MSc thesis direction
In progress

Class-Incremental Learning with Modality-Aware Representation Primitives

The thesis investigates whether modality-aware representations can make continual medical-imaging systems more robust across sequential tasks. The planned work develops task-conditioned CT representations, MRI pulse-sequence-aware representations, and continual-learning evaluations centered on retention, forward transfer, and task performance.

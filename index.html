Current work

Learning to Window: Context-Aware HU Adaptation for Continual Trauma Injury Detection in CT Scans

This project studies whether task-adaptive CT preprocessing improves continual trauma detection beyond standard fixed-window baselines. It combines differentiable CT windowing with sequential organ-level learning and targeted ablations that separate the effect of window initialization, smooth intensity transforms, learnability, and continual-learning strategy.
